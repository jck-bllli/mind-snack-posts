# Night Town, 30 second script (draft 2)

**Format:** 1080x1920 vertical, 30 fps, one continuous first-person shot with no cuts. Same look as the 5s clip: purple cobblestone town, orange windows, swirly sky, embers.
**Captions:** none. The story is all animation and sound.
**Voice:** none. Synth score only.
**Focus:** heavy on animation. Something in frame is always moving or transforming.
**Loop:** we fly into the creature's mouth and come out at the start of the same street, so the reel replays seamlessly.

| Time | What you see | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4s | Slow sleepwalk down the street. The houses breathe: walls swell in and out and roofs bend like rubber. Chimneys puff purple smoke rings that drift up and spin. | Music box, soft pad |
| 4 to 8s | Lanterns pop off their posts one by one and float up like glowing jellyfish, trailing embers. The cobblestones ripple under our feet like a wave rolling down the street. | Music box gets wobbly, airy whooshes |
| 8 to 12s | The town wakes up. Every window blinks like an eye in a wave down the street, and doors open and shut like mouths. Houses on both sides fold flat and pop back up like a pop-up book as we pass. | Clicks and thumps in rhythm, low hum |
| 12 to 16s | The street stretches ahead like taffy and the houses lean in to form an arch. The sky spirals faster and stars streak into rings. The giant horned head rises over the end of the street, eyes shut. | Rumble builds, music box stops |
| 16s | Its yellow slit eyes snap open. Every window in town turns off at the same instant. | Deep hit and dissonant tone |
| 16 to 21s | Its horns grow and curl. The glowing blue markings on its head crawl across its skin like lightning. Floating lanterns orbit its head like moons. | Pad swells, heartbeat thump |
| 21 to 26s | The grin opens wider and wider until the mouth fills the screen. Inside the mouth is the same town, tiny and glowing. The camera keeps walking straight into it. | Rising whoosh |
| 26 to 30s | We fall through the mouth and land on the street from the first shot. Lanterns are back on their posts and the houses are still. This is the loop point. | Music box first note, loops |

## Notes
- The creature is the original one from the 5s clip (blue scaly head, pale horns, yellow slit eyes, orange grin). Nothing copied from the example or any game.
- No jumpscare. The eye-open moment is a beat, not a scream.
- Renders on the home PC GPU and comes back here automatically.
- Sample frames first (breathing houses, jellyfish lanterns, eyes open, into the mouth), then the full render.
