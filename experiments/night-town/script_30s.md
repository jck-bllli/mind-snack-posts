# Night Town, 30 second script (draft 1)

**Format:** 1080x1920 vertical, 30 fps, one continuous first-person walk with no cuts. Same look as the 5s clip: purple cobblestone town, orange windows, swirly sky, embers.
**Voice:** none. On-screen captions in a plain white sleepy-text style, lowercase, one line at a time (like your example).
**Sound:** synth score only (music box, dark pad, rumble, hits).
**Loop:** the last frame matches the first, so the reel replays seamlessly.

| Time | What you see | Caption | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4s | Slow walk down the empty street. Lanterns flicker, embers drift. | "it's 3am. i just want to go home and sleep" | Music box, soft pad |
| 4 to 8s | Behind each lantern we pass, its light goes out with a soft *pff*. The street gets darker toward the camera. | "why are the lights turning off" | Music box slows down and goes slightly out of tune |
| 8 to 12s | Every window on both sides turns to look at you: the orange squares blink like eyes, one after another down the street. | "the houses are awake" | Low rumble starts |
| 12 to 16s | At the end of the road the giant horned head rises over the rooftops, eyes still shut. Ground shakes slightly. | "oh no" | Rumble builds |
| 16s | Its yellow slit eyes snap open. | (no caption, hold) | Deep hit + dissonant tone |
| 16 to 21s | Slow push in. The glowing orange grin widens. Its eyes follow the camera. | "it's not mad. it just wants to know why you're still up" | Pad swells, heartbeat thump |
| 21 to 25s | The creature slowly yawns. Its eyelids get heavy. The windows dim one by one. | "...fair point" | Music box returns, slower |
| 25 to 28s | The creature sinks back behind the roofs, closing its eyes. Lanterns relight in a wave toward the camera. | "goodnight" | Everything softens |
| 28 to 30s | Back to the same empty street as 0s, lanterns lit. Seamless loop point. | (fade caption out) | Music box first note, loops |

## Notes
- The creature is the original one from the 5s clip (blue scaly head, pale horns, yellow slit eyes, orange grin). Nothing copied from the example or any game.
- No jumpscare: the reveal is slow and the creature turns friendly-sleepy.
